## Spec

**Problem:** The first P0 pass solved hover dependence and the moving Discord
target, but compressed too much information into bordered, dashboard-like
panels. Project cards now feel text-heavy and undersized on desktop, while the
join section's nested boxes and dense onboarding copy do not match the site's
spacious, atmospheric space-editorial language.

**Approach:** Keep P0 accessibility and recruiting content while changing its
visual hierarchy. Project cards become taller on desktop and icon-led, with a
short purpose line, two facts, one CTA, project accent glow, and deliberate
negative space; mobile height stays at the audit's 280px ceiling. The join
section becomes one open, full-width composition: an orbital Discord visual,
one short invitation, two CTAs, a lightweight three-step rail, and a single
meeting-status line. Remove redundant explanatory prose and nested dashboard
surfaces. Record the new component convention in the established design system.
