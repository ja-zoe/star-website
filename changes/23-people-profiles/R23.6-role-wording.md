# R23.6 - Role wording

**Status:** done
**Files:** `src/components/project/ProjectShell.tsx`; `src/routes/team/TeamPage.tsx`;
`scripts/prerender-routes.mjs`; comments in `src/components/people/`; `design/components.md`

## Spec

**Problem (user feedback, 2026-10-05):** "The people steering {project}." and "Open anyone to see
everything they do." read poorly. "Lead" means a subteam lead in STAR, someone who leads the
people working on one system, so project managers and chief engineers must not be described as
leads or leadership. Nothing should suggest they rank above subteam leads.

**Approach:**
- Project pages: drop the subheading. Section 05's label names the roles it holds, built from the
  data: "Project manager & chief engineer" (CubeSat), "Project managers & chief engineer"
  (Robotics), "Project manager" (Weather Balloon). Anchor `#leadership` -> `#project-management`.
- `/team` description: "Click or press on anyone to see their profile." (user's wording).
- Team share card tagline: "E-board. Project managers. Chief engineers. Subteam leads."
- Not changed: "Maintained by STAR <project> leadership" (set 19 `contentOwner`), which names who
  maintains page content, not these roles. Flagged to the user.
