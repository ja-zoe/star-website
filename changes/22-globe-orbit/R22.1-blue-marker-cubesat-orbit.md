# R22.1 — Blue Rutgers marker and perspective CubeSat orbit

**Status:** in progress
**Files:** `src/components/magicui/globe.tsx`

## Spec

**Problem:** The Rutgers marker is orange/red and the globe remains visually isolated despite being
one of the homepage's strongest spatial elements. The user wants a blue location dot and an object
that visibly orbits the globe in three dimensions.

**Approach:** Change cobe's live marker and the deterministic reduced-motion DOM marker to the
Weather Balloon sky blue (`#38BDF8`). Add one decorative CubeSat glyph following a mathematically
projected circular orbit: tilt and roll the orbit plane, apply depth-based perspective scaling and
brightness, split the orbit track into subdued rear and brighter front arcs, and place the moving
glyph behind or in front of the globe based on its projected depth. Reuse the globe's existing
IntersectionObserver, document visibility, and global motion preference. Cap the orbit compositor at
30 fps. In reduced motion, show a complete static front-side orbit composition while preserving the
Rutgers-facing globe and visible blue marker. Keep globe dragging and the About section layout/copy
unchanged. Use `lucide-react`'s Satellite glyph; add no package or second WebGL canvas.
