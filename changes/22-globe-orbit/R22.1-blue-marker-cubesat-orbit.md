# R22.1 — Blue Rutgers marker and perspective CubeSat orbit

**Status:** tests passing
**Files:** `src/components/magicui/globe.tsx`

## Spec

**Problem:** The Rutgers marker is orange/red and the globe remains visually isolated despite being
one of the homepage's strongest spatial elements. The orbit concept should feel like a mission
visual, not a flat decorative ellipse or pointed star icon.

**Approach:** Change cobe's live marker and the deterministic reduced-motion DOM marker to the
Weather Balloon sky blue (`#38BDF8`). Project one circular low-Earth orbit from 3D coordinates into
the SVG view, drawing only its camera-facing half so the track disappears behind Earth. Render the
track as depth-weighted segments so it is subdued overall and fades toward both horizons. Add a
small bright circular tracer with a soft radial falloff; hide it on the far side and vary its scale
and opacity with depth. Do not add secondary orbit paths or an orbit text label. Reuse the globe's existing
IntersectionObserver, document visibility, and global motion preference. Cap the orbit compositor at
30 fps. In reduced motion, show a complete static front-side orbit composition while preserving the
Rutgers-facing globe and visible blue marker. Keep globe dragging and the About section layout/copy
unchanged. Use CSS/SVG for the tracer and orbit paths; add no package or second WebGL canvas.
