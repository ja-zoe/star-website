## Spec

**Problem:** Recruitment and meeting copy is indefinite, project pages provide
no dated logistics/recruitment context, and the sponsor area presents unlike
relationships as seven equivalent sponsors.

**Approach:** Add one typed static source of truth with Fall 2026, last-updated
2026-08-29, schedule-pending state, usual location, email/Discord confirmation,
open-interest status, no-prior-experience baseline, and per-project schedule
fallbacks. Surface it in the hero status strip, project closing sections, and a
current-missions snapshot. Split partners from About, rename to "Partners,
programs & supporters," label each relationship (institution, chapter network,
program, competition, student governance, software partner), and add a sponsor
inquiry mailto CTA. Do not assert unpublished dates, rooms, commitments, or
results.
