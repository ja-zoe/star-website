## Spec

**Problem:** The P1 information architecture works, but its new visual layer
removed recognizable STAR effects and introduced generic grid/glow motifs that
read as generated rather than authored.

hero, project-card, join, leadership, and partner treatments. Use installed
Aceternity primitives rather than rebuilding effects ad hoc. Keep Space Mono
and STAR’s existing starfield/shooting-star shell. Provide a local motion toggle
that also respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not alter the production home.
