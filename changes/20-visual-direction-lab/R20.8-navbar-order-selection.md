# R20.8 — Navbar order and transient manual selection

**Status:** done
**Files:** `src/components/Navbar.tsx`, `src/App.css`

## Spec

**Problem:** The navbar lists FAQ before Join Us even though Join Us appears first in the page
flow. Pointer-clicked links also retain the coral focus glow after the scroll-aware active section
moves elsewhere, creating two persistent selections without an active hover.

**Approach:** Place Join Us before FAQ in both desktop and mobile navigation. Remove ordinary
mouse focus from the coral exploration selector while retaining `:focus-visible` for keyboard
users. Override the Radix Projects open state to a neutral background and shadow so opening the
menu cannot restore shadcn's light accent; active and hovered/focus-visible states continue to win
through the existing scoped selectors. Do not alter navbar geometry, spacing, scroll calculation,
or the user's other navbar changes.
